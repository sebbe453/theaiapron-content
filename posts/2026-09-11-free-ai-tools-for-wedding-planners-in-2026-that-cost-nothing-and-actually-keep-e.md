## Free AI Tools Worth Using in 2026

### ChatGPT (Free Tier)
**Cost:** Free (GPT-4o mini on the free plan)
**Best for:** Drafting client emails, vendor communications, social media captions, and building run-of-day timelines

You type: *"Write a polite follow-up email to a florist who hasn't confirmed the ceremony arch delivery."* You get a professional, warm, ready-to-personalise draft in about eight seconds.

For wedding planners specifically, ChatGPT is most useful for the email volume. In a busy week, you might send 40–60 emails. A significant portion of those follow a pattern. Let AI draft the pattern; you add the personal detail.

**Honest limitation:** The free tier limits how many GPT-4o messages you can send in a given window before dropping to a slower model. On a heavy-admin day, you may hit that ceiling. It's not a dealbreaker — it just means front-loading your drafting sessions rather than going back and forth all day.

### Notion AI (Free Tier — AI features available on paid plans, but Notion itself is free for personal use)
**Cost:** Notion base is free; AI add-on is £8/month — but you can use the free Notion without AI features as a base, and combine with free ChatGPT for AI-assisted content inside it
**Best for:** Building wedding planning templates, master timelines, and per-couple project trackers

Here's the practical move: use free Notion to organise every wedding — one page per couple, with vendor lists, budget trackers, timeline, and communication logs. Then use free ChatGPT alongside it to generate the content that goes inside those pages.

You end up with a system that looks professional, keeps everything in one place, and costs nothing.

**Honest limitation:** Notion has a learning curve. If you've never used it, expect to spend two to three hours building your template before it saves you time. It pays back fast, but the setup isn't instant.

### Google Gemini (Free)
**Cost:** Free
**Best for:** Summarising long email threads, drafting FAQs for couples, and generating venue or supplier comparison notes

If a couple has sent you a 40-message email thread about catering options and you need to pull the key decisions out of it, Gemini handles that well. Paste the thread, ask it to summarise the outstanding decisions, and you have a clear action list in under a minute.

It also integrates with Google Workspace, which many planners already use. That makes it practical without requiring any new systems.

**Honest limitation:** Gemini's output can be generic if your prompt is vague. The more specific your request — including names, context, and tone — the better the result. It's not a one-click tool; it rewards people who learn to prompt well.

### Canva AI (Free Tier)
**Cost:** Free (with limits); Canva Pro is £13/month if you want the full AI suite
**Best for:** Creating branded client welcome guides, mood board templates, and social graphics

The free tier includes AI-assisted design suggestions and basic text-to-image generation. For a wedding planner, the highest-value use is creating client-facing documents — welcome packs, planning guides, seating chart templates — that look polished without requiring design skills.

A real example: a planner in Bristol told us she used to spend 45 minutes per couple personalising a welcome pack in Word. With Canva templates, she does the same job in 12 minutes and the result looks significantly better.

**Honest limitation:** The free tier watermarks some AI-generated images and limits how many AI image credits you get per month. For document design and templates — which don't require AI-generated images — the free tier is genuinely sufficient.

### Otter.ai (Free Tier)
**Cost:** Free for up to 300 minutes of transcription per month
**Best for:** Transcribing client consultations, supplier calls, and venue walkthroughs

Record your initial consultation with a couple (with their permission), let Otter transcribe it, and you have a written record of everything they said they wanted, expected, and worried about. That's a reference document you can return to for the next twelve months.

It also means you can stop furiously taking notes mid-meeting and actually be present with your clients — which they notice.

**Honest limitation:** Otter's free tier caps at 300 minutes per month and 30 minutes per conversation. If you do more than 10 consultations a month, you'll hit the ceiling. For lower-volume planners or those using it selectively, the free tier is enough.

## How to Get Started (Assume Zero Tech Knowledge)

1. **Start with one email you write every week.** Pick a recurring message — a supplier check-in, a 30-day countdown note to a couple, a venue confirmation request — and use free ChatGPT to draft it. Send it with your own tweaks. That's your first win.

2. **Set up a free Notion account and create one couple's project page.** Use ChatGPT to help you build the structure: ask it "give me a wedding planning checklist template broken down by months before the wedding." Copy the output into Notion and adapt it.

3. **Download Otter.ai on your phone before your next client call.** Open it, tap record, and let it run. Afterwards, read the transcript. You'll immediately see the value.

4. **Create one piece of client-facing content in Canva.** Start with a welcome guide or a "what happens next" document. Use an existing Canva template — the design work is already done. Just replace the text and colours with your brand.

5. **Spend 20 minutes learning one prompt formula.** The best starting point: "Write a [type of message] to [who] about [specific situation], in a [warm/professional/concise] tone." That single formula will cover 80% of your AI drafting needs.

## Realistic Expectations: Timeline, Results, and What Can Go Wrong

**Setup time:** Two to four hours across your first week if you're building a Notion system from scratch. Using just ChatGPT for email drafting takes about 15 minutes to get comfortable with.

**When you'll feel the difference:** Most planners notice the time saving within the first week of using AI for email drafts. The bigger structural savings — from having everything organised in Notion — take two to three weeks to feel significant.

**What can go wrong:** AI drafts emails in your name, not your voice. If you paste a generic prompt, you'll get a generic result that sounds nothing like you. Every draft needs your eye before it sends — that's not optional. Also, don't use AI to draft anything emotionally sensitive without reading it carefully. A message to a couple dealing with a family bereavement or a vendor dispute needs your judgment, not an algorithm's.

**Cost risk:** Zero. Every tool listed here has a functional free tier. The paid versions exist if you scale up, but you don't need them to start.
