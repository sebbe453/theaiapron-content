# AI Customer Service Tools for Opticians in 2026 (That Handle the Appointment Queries, Recall Reminders, and "Do I Need a New Prescription?" Messages While You're Testing Eyes)

---

Your reception desk is managing walk-in enquiries, answering the phone, responding to Facebook messages, and manually chasing patients who were due a recall six months ago — all at the same time. Something gets dropped every single day.

## Why Optician Reception Desks Are Under So Much Pressure

The problem isn't that your team is disorganised. It's that the volume of repetitive, low-complexity communication is relentless — and it all lands in the same place as your genuine clinical emergencies.

A patient rings to ask what your contact lens fitting fee is. Someone messages on Instagram asking if you do children's eye tests. Another person hasn't responded to their recall letter and needs a follow-up. None of these tasks require a trained optical assistant, but they all consume time that your trained optical assistant doesn't have.

Meanwhile, the patients most likely to actually book — the ones who enquire after hours or on a Saturday — get a wall of silence until Monday morning, when half of them have already called the optician down the road.

## What AI Customer Service Tools Actually Do for Opticians

AI customer service tools sit between your patients and your team. They respond instantly to common enquiries, qualify what a patient actually needs, and either resolve it automatically or pass it to the right person with the context already gathered.

For an optician specifically, that means a patient can message at 7pm asking about progressive lens upgrades and get a useful, accurate response — not a generic "we'll be in touch." It means your recall list doesn't just sit in a spreadsheet; it generates automated, personalised follow-ups that go out without anyone on your team lifting a finger. It means the question "how long does an eye test take?" stops being something your receptionist answers eight times a day.

The difference between a good AI tool and a bad one, in this context, is specificity. A generic chatbot that says "thanks for your message, someone will be in touch" adds no value. The tools below are built to actually handle the query — or at least move it forward meaningfully before a human gets involved.

## The Tools to Use

### Tidio
**Cost:** Free plan available. Paid plans from $29/month (Lyro AI plan from $39/month).
**Best for:** Handling website enquiries — booking questions, pricing, services, opening hours — through a live chat and AI hybrid.
**How it works for opticians:** You set up Tidio on your website and train it with your practice's specific information: your services, fees, appointment types, lens brands you stock, and FAQs. When a patient visits your site at 9pm and asks "do you offer driving vision assessments?", Tidio answers immediately and can direct them to book. The Lyro AI component handles conversation naturally rather than just matching keywords.
**Honest limitation:** Tidio works best on your website. If most of your patient communication happens on WhatsApp or Facebook Messenger, you'll need to connect those channels separately, and the setup takes a bit of time to get right. It also can't access your actual diary — it can't confirm specific appointment slots without an integration.

### Manychat
**Cost:** Free plan available (limited contacts). Pro plan from $15/month.
**Best for:** Automating Instagram DMs and Facebook Messenger — the channels where you're currently getting enquiries and not responding fast enough.
**How it works for opticians:** A patient DMs your practice on Instagram asking about varifocal lenses. Manychat responds instantly with the information they need, asks qualifying questions ("are you an existing patient?"), and either answers fully or sends a booking link. You can also use it to run recall campaigns — message everyone who hasn't booked in two years with a targeted offer.
**Honest limitation:** Manychat is powerful but it takes a few hours to build your flows properly. If you set it up half-finished, patients will hit dead ends in the conversation and get frustrated. Budget time upfront to do it properly, or it'll make you look less organised, not more.

### Cliniko (with automation features)
**Cost:** From $45/month for small practices. Pricing scales by practitioner.
**Best for:** Appointment reminders, recall campaigns, and reducing no-shows — directly integrated with your booking system.
**How it works for opticians:** Cliniko is a practice management platform with built-in automation. It sends appointment reminders via SMS or email automatically, flags overdue recalls, and follows up with patients who don't respond. You set the rules once — "send a reminder 48 hours before, send a follow-up if they don't confirm" — and it runs without you.
**Honest limitation:** Cliniko is a practice management system first, not a customer service chatbot. It won't handle freeform enquiries. If you want to manage Instagram messages or website chat, you'll need a separate tool alongside it. Also, migrating from your current system takes time and patience.

## How to Get Started (Assume Zero Tech Experience)

1. **Pick one channel first.** Don't try to automate everything at once. Look at where your unanswered messages are piling up — website, Instagram, SMS — and start there. One tool, one channel.

2. **Write down your 10 most common patient questions.** Things like: "How much is an eye test?", "Do you do NHS tests?", "How long does an appointment take?", "Can my child come in without an adult?" These become the foundation of your AI's training.

3. **Set up the tool and feed it your answers.** For Tidio or Manychat, this means entering your FAQs and creating simple response flows. For Cliniko, it means importing your patient list and setting your reminder rules. Most of these tools have guided setup wizards — follow them.

4. **Test it yourself.** Before you go live, message your own chatbot or trigger your own reminder. Walk through it as a patient would. Fix anything that sounds robotic, unhelpful, or incomplete.

5. **Turn it on and check the conversations daily for the first two weeks.** AI tools learn what works and what doesn't. The first two weeks will show you gaps — questions you didn't anticipate, responses that need refining. After that, maintenance drops to once or twice a month.

## Realistic Expectations: What Changes and When

**Setup time:** Tidio or Manychat takes 2–4 hours to set up properly if you're starting from scratch. Cliniko is a bigger commitment — expect a few days to get fully migrated and configured. Intercom sits somewhere in the middle.

**When you'll notice a difference:** Within the first week, you'll see enquiries being handled without your team touching them. Within a month, you should see a measurable reduction in the volume of repetitive inbound messages — and your recall response rate should improve if you've automated that.

**What can go wrong:** The most common failure is under-training the tool. If you only add five FAQs and your patients ask questions you haven't covered, the AI either gives a wrong answer or falls back on something generic. The fix is simple — keep adding to it as new questions come in. The second issue is forgetting to tell patients you have it. If your website chat widget is buried at the bottom of your contact page, nobody will use it. Put it somewhere visible.
