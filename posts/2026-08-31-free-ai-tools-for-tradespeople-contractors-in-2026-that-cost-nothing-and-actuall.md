## The Free AI Tools Worth Using in 2026

### ChatGPT (Free Plan)

**Cost:** Free (GPT-4o access included on the free tier as of 2026)

**Best for:** Writing quotes, follow-up emails, job descriptions for subcontractors, Google review responses, and social media captions.

**How to use it:** Type in plain English what you need. "Write a professional follow-up email to a customer who asked for a quote on a new boiler three days ago but hasn't replied." It gives you a ready-to-send draft in seconds.

**Honest limitation:** The free plan has usage limits, so if you're hitting it hard across a busy week, you may get slowed down or temporarily locked out until the next day. It also doesn't connect to your actual calendar or accounts — everything is manual copy-paste.

---

### Google Gemini (Free)

**Cost:** Free via gemini.google.com or built directly into Gmail

**Best for:** Drafting professional emails inside Gmail, summarising long email threads, and helping you write calm, measured responses to difficult customer messages without sounding defensive.

**How to use it:** If you use Gmail — which most small businesses do — Gemini is already built in. Open any email draft, click the "Help me write" button, and describe what you want in a sentence or two. It produces a full draft you can edit and send.

**Honest limitation:** Gemini works best when you already know what you want to say and just need help saying it clearly. For creative tasks or more complex writing — like building a full quote template from scratch — the free tier is noticeably less capable than ChatGPT. It's an email assistant first, a general writing tool second.

---

### Canva (Free Plan) with AI Features

**Cost:** Free — paid plan (Canva Pro) costs £13/month and unlocks more AI features, but the free plan is genuinely useful on its own.

**Best for:** Creating before-and-after posts, job completion cards, simple service flyers, and professional-looking visuals without any design background.

**How to use it:** Upload your site photos, choose a template, and use Canva's Magic Resize to format them for Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp in one click. The free "Text to Image" tool can generate background visuals when you don't have a photo to hand.

**Honest limitation:** The most useful AI writing features — Magic Write for longer copy and the Brand Kit for consistent colours and fonts — are locked behind the paid plan. The free version handles visuals well but won't help much with writing your captions or post copy. Pair it with ChatGPT for the words, use Canva for the images.

---

### Microsoft Copilot (Free)

**Cost:** Free via copilot.microsoft.com and as a Windows app — no Microsoft 365 subscription required for the basic version.

**Best for:** Generating quote templates in Word format, writing professional emails, and summarising long documents like supplier contracts or terms and conditions you don't have time to read in full.

**How to use it:** Go to copilot.microsoft.com and type your request in plain English. "Create a professional quote template for a plastering business that includes materials, labour, VAT, and payment terms." It builds the full document structure for you, which you can copy into Word or Google Docs and customise.

**Honest limitation:** Microsoft Copilot is solid but not particularly sharp on creative or nuanced writing — its outputs can feel dry and corporate without some editing on your part. It integrates well if you're already using Microsoft 365, but as a standalone tool it's more functional than flexible. If you want the best writing quality, ChatGPT edges it.

---

### Notion AI (Free Tier)

**Cost:** Free for basic Notion use; AI features are limited to a set number of uses per month before prompting you to upgrade. The paid AI add-on costs £8/month on top of the Notion plan.

**Best for:** Keeping a running quote log, job notes, customer records, and follow-up reminders all in one place — with AI helping you tidy rough notes into professional job summaries.

**How to use it:** Create a simple "Jobs" database in Notion. After every site visit, dump your rough notes in — even just a voice-to-text transcript from your phone. Use Notion AI to turn those rough notes into a clean job summary you can copy straight into a quote email.

**Honest limitation:** The free AI usage runs out quickly if you're using it daily — you'll likely hit the monthly limit within one to two weeks of regular use. It's also a more involved setup than the other tools on this list. If you only need a simple notes system, a phone notes app costs nothing and requires no setup at all. Notion earns its place once your job volume grows and you need proper organisation, not just writing help.

## How to Get Started with Free AI Tools for Tradespeople (Zero Tech Knowledge Required)

1. **Pick one tool, not five.** If you use Gmail, start with Google Gemini — it's already inside your inbox, no new account needed. If you don't use Gmail, go to chat.openai.com and create a free ChatGPT account with your email address. Five minutes, and you're in.

2. **Identify your single worst admin task.** Is it writing quotes? Chasing customers who've gone quiet? Responding to bad reviews? Pick the one that costs you the most time or the most stress, and use your chosen tool for that task first — nothing else.

3. **Describe what you need in plain English.** Don't overthink the wording. "I need a follow-up message to send to someone who asked for a quote on a kitchen extension two weeks ago but hasn't responded" is a perfect prompt. The AI will handle the structure, tone, and wording.

4. **Read it, tweak it, send it.** Never send anything AI-generated without reading it first. It'll be 80–90% right out of the box. You add the job-specific details, make sure the tone sounds like you rather than a call centre, and send.

5. **Save the prompts that work.** When you find a prompt that gets you a great result, copy it into your phone notes. Over a few weeks, you'll build a small library of shortcuts that makes every task faster — a quote follow-up prompt, a Google review response prompt, a social post prompt for completed jobs.

## Realistic Expectations: What to Know Before You Start

**Setup time:** 20–30 minutes to create an account and get comfortable with your first tool. Less if you use something already built into Gmail or Windows.

**When you'll see results:** Most tradespeople notice time savings from day one on quote writing and follow-up emails. A more consistent, professional-looking social media presence takes a couple of weeks of regular use before it starts having any visible effect on enquiries.

**What can go wrong:** AI writes for the average — which means its first draft can sound slightly generic. A quote email that reads like it came from a faceless corporation rather than a trusted local tradesperson won't land as well as one that sounds like you. The fix is straightforward: read it, personalise it, add the specific job details. Don't copy and paste blind.

The free tiers are genuinely useful but have real limits. If you find yourself hitting usage caps mid-week, that's usually a sign the tool is actually working for you — and it might be worth paying for. ChatGPT Plus costs £18/month and removes most of the restrictions. But there's no pressure to upgrade until you've proven the free version is saving you time.
