# AI Customer Service Tools for Tradespeople & Contractors in 2026 (That Handle the Enquiries, Quote Follow-Ups, and "Are You Available?" Messages While You're Actually on the Job)

---

You missed three enquiries last Tuesday because you were under a sink from 8am to 5pm. One of them went with someone else by Wednesday morning.

That's not a sales problem. That's a response-time problem — and AI can fix it without you touching your phone once.

---

## Why Tradespeople Lose Work to Slower Competitors

The average homeowner contacts two or three tradespeople at once. Whoever replies first — or at least replies the same day — gets the job most of the time. You already know this.

The problem is that you physically cannot respond while you're driving between jobs, working on site, or halfway through a bathroom fit. So enquiries sit. People assume you're not interested or just too busy. They move on.

This isn't laziness on your part. It's the reality of working in a trade. You're using your hands, not a keyboard.

---

## What AI Customer Service Tools Actually Do for Tradespeople

AI customer service tools sit between you and your enquiries. When someone messages your website, Facebook page, or Google profile, the AI responds immediately — asking the right questions, giving your pricing ballpark, and collecting contact details.

By the time you finish the job and check your phone, you don't have a cold enquiry to chase. You have a qualified lead with a name, address, job type, and availability already written up. That's the difference.

These tools also handle follow-ups on quotes you've already sent. If someone hasn't replied in 48 hours, the AI sends a polite nudge. You don't have to remember to chase, and you don't lose jobs simply because you forgot to follow up.

One plumber in Sheffield started using a chatbot on his website last year. He went from converting roughly 3 out of 10 enquiries to 6 out of 10 — not because his prices changed, but because no enquiry went unanswered for more than four minutes, even at 11pm.

## Which AI Customer Service Tools Work Best for Tradespeople?

### Tidio
**Cost:** Free plan available; paid plans from £29/month
**Best for:** Tradespeople with a website who want a chatbot to capture enquiries 24/7
**Honest limitation:** The free plan limits you to 50 chat conversations per month — enough to test it, but you'll need the paid version if you're busy

[Visit Tidio](https://www.tidio.com)

Tidio sits on your website and greets visitors automatically. You can set it up to ask: "What kind of job do you need doing?" and then walk them through a short set of questions — location, job size, rough timing — before collecting their number.

You get a notification with the full conversation summary. No missed enquiry, no cold call, no "I messaged you three days ago" awkwardness.

### Manychat
**Cost:** Free plan available; Pro plan from £13/month
**Best for:** Tradespeople who get most of their enquiries through Facebook, Instagram, or Google Business
**Honest limitation:** Manychat works on social platforms, not your website — if your leads come mainly from Google searches landing on your site, you'll need something else alongside it

[Manychat](https://www.google.com/search?q=Manychat+pricing+review)

If you've ever posted a job photo on Facebook and had 15 people comment "how much?" or "are you available in March?" — Manychat handles those replies automatically.

You set a trigger word (like "quote" or "available") and Manychat sends a private message asking for their details. You wake up in the morning with a list of real leads instead of a comment thread you forgot to check.

---

### Recapped
**Cost:** From £29/month
**Best for:** Contractors who send formal quotes and need to automate follow-ups and client sign-off
**Honest limitation:** Recapped is built for quoting and proposals, not for first-contact customer service — it works best after the enquiry is already captured, not as a standalone lead response tool

[Recapped](https://www.google.com/search?q=Recapped+pricing+review)

Recapped sends your quote as a professional, trackable link. You can see when someone opens it, and it follows up automatically if they don't respond. You can also set up a simple approval process so clients sign off digitally — no printing, no chasing.

For bigger jobs where you're sending detailed estimates, this alone can save you an hour a week.

---

### Signpost
**Cost:** From £199/month
**Best for:** Established trade businesses with steady enquiry volume who want reviews, follow-ups, and lead response all managed in one place
**Honest limitation:** The price puts it out of reach for sole traders just starting out — this one makes sense only if you're already turning over enough to justify a monthly tool at that level

[Signpost](https://www.google.com/search?q=Signpost+pricing+review)

Signpost combines auto-response to new enquiries, automated review requests after jobs, and follow-up sequences for cold leads. It's not cheap, but for a multi-van trade business it can replace several separate tools and the admin hours that go with them.

If you're a one-person operation, start with Tidio or Manychat first. Come back to Signpost when your volume makes the price an easy decision.

### ChatGPT (via Zapier Integration)
**Cost:** ChatGPT Plus from £16/month; Zapier from £19.99/month (separate subscriptions)
**Best for:** Tradespeople who want custom automated responses without paying for specialist software — especially if they're already using ChatGPT for other tasks
**Honest limitation:** This takes more setup than the plug-and-play options above — if you want something working this week with no trial and error, start with Tidio or Manychat instead

[Zapier](https://www.google.com/search?q=Zapier+pricing+review)

Using Zapier, you can connect a contact form on your website to ChatGPT, which drafts a personalised first response based on what the customer typed — then sends it automatically from your email address.

It sounds complicated but there are templates inside Zapier that do most of the work. The advantage is total flexibility and lower combined cost if you're already paying for ChatGPT for writing quotes, job descriptions, or anything else.

## How to Get Started (Assume You've Never Done This Before)

1. **Identify where your enquiries come from first.** Is it mainly your website, Facebook, a Google Business profile, or word of mouth via WhatsApp? Pick the channel that brings the most leads and start there — not everywhere at once.

2. **Sign up for the right tool for that channel.** Website enquiries → Tidio. Facebook or Instagram → Manychat. Formal quote follow-up → Recapped. All-in-one with reviews included → Signpost if your budget allows. Custom and flexible → ChatGPT via Zapier.

3. **Write three pieces of copy before you launch anything.** You need: a greeting message, a set of three or four qualifying questions (job type, location, timing, rough budget), and a closing message that tells them you'll be in touch within a set timeframe. These become your chatbot script.

4. **Test it yourself first.** Go to your own Facebook page or website and act like a customer. Send a message. See what the bot says. Fix anything that sounds robotic or like it was written by a call centre.

5. **Check your leads once a day at the same time.** Set a fixed window — 7am with your coffee, or 6pm after your last job — to review what the AI has captured. Respond personally to the best ones. The AI handles the rest.

## Realistic Expectations: What Will Actually Happen and When

**Setup time:** Tidio or Manychat can be live within two hours if you follow their setup guides. Signpost or a Zapier-ChatGPT setup will take a full weekend afternoon, possibly two sessions.

**When you'll notice results:** Most tradespeople see a difference within the first week — specifically in the number of enquiries they actually respond to same-day. Whether those convert to booked jobs depends on your pricing and availability, not the tool.

**What can go wrong:**

- If your chatbot asks too many questions upfront, people drop off before they finish. Keep qualifying questions to three or four at most.
- If the automated message sounds like a corporate script, people won't trust it. Write it the way you'd actually speak to someone — casual, direct, no jargon.
- If you never check your leads dashboard, you'll still miss jobs. The AI captures the lead; you still have to close it.

**One thing no AI tool will fix:** If your reviews are weak, your pricing is unclear, or your website looks like it was last updated in 2014, better lead response won't save you. Sort those first, then add the automation on top.
